# Accelerator Memory

Language for the accelerator’s continuity and evidence selection. Canonical project sources retain authority over retrieved material.

## Language

**Project Brain**:
Shared records of active work, findings, decisions and their evidence.

**Memory Bank**:
Durable knowledge promoted from eligible evidence, with provenance and a review lifecycle.

**Context capsule**:
A bounded selection of working context and eligible evidence for a particular request and session.

**Evidence term form**:
The lexical form used to compare request words with evidence excerpts and related history. It is a matching aid, not a claim that two pieces of evidence mean the same thing.

**Run Working Memory**:
Short-lived observations, open questions and hypotheses shared within one run. They remain worker claims with provenance and expiry, rather than verified reusable knowledge.
